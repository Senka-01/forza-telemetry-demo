      Reading this view

      Throttle and brake overlays represent pedal inputs, not acceleration force. Values between recorded samples are interpolated. Onsets mark transitions from at most 5% to above 5%; they are sampled, not exact braking distances. Sector lines use the selected lap's estimated traveled distance, so their locations may differ between laps. Corner times compare the same map locations on both laps (matched by position, so they don't depend on each lap's measured distance). A corner's time covers the stretch from halfway after the previous corner to halfway before the next, so the corner times add up to the lap-time difference when every stretch could be matched. Approach markers count back from where the corner starts to turn the car. Different driving lines and missing samples limit accuracy; a faster segment does not establish cause. Off-track detection uses the game's per-tire surface rumble and rumble-strip flags: a tire is off when its rumble is above the threshold while it is not on a kerb. Tire times add up each tire separately, so they can exceed the event time.
